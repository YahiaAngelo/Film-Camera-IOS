# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 25, 2026_

Film Camera does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## Camera

Film Camera uses the camera only to show the live preview and take the photos you ask for. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded.

## Photos

Photos you take are saved to your photo library, with your permission. Film Camera asks only for permission to **add** photos. It cannot read the rest of your library.

## Network

Film Camera connects to GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) only to download the film catalog, film LUT files and their preview images. These requests contain no personal information. GitHub may log standard request data such as IP addresses under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Analytics, tracking and advertising

Film Camera contains no analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings, your film list and downloaded films are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/YahiaAngelo/Film-Camera/issues.
